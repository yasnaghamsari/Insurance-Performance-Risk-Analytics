Tell Me About Your Project (1–2 minutes)

My project is an Insurance Portfolio Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI.

The objective of the project was to analyze insurance business performance and convert raw insurance data into meaningful business insights for decision making.

I worked with multiple datasets containing customer details, policy information, premium transactions, claims data, and agent information. First, I imported the Excel data into Power BI and performed data cleaning in Power Query by fixing data types, correcting date formats, and preparing the dataset for analysis.

After that, I built a Star Schema data model by creating relationships between dimension and fact tables to improve performance and make reporting easier.

Then I created DAX measures to calculate important KPIs such as Total Premium, Total Claims Amount, Loss Ratio, Claim Approval Rate, and Policy Lapse Rate.

Finally, I designed a 4-page interactive dashboard covering Executive Overview, Claims Analysis, Agent Performance, and Risk & Retention.

One of the major insights I found was that the portfolio had a Loss Ratio of 106%, meaning claims exceeded premiums, which indicated profitability concerns and highlighted the need for business improvement.
